COMPETITION RULES & STRUCTURE

Detailed Segment Guidelines

Comprehensive evaluation criteria and stage breakdown for all championship segments.

INDIVIDUAL SEGMENT 01

1. Grand Title Championship (Batch Olympiad & Title Path)

Phase A — Batch-wise Olympiad

All registered participants compete within their respective academic batches. Based on merit, the Top 10 participants from each batch win regional recognition and qualify directly for Phase B.

Phase B — Title Path Written Test

The batch Top 10 finalists take a specialized 1-Hour Examination covering Vocabulary, Advanced Spelling Tests, and Creative Writing.

Titles & Awards

The highest scoring male student earns the title Mr. Englishpedia 2026, and the top female student earns Miss. Englishpedia 2026. Winners receive the Champions Trophy, Cash Prize, Winner Certificates, and an official Stage Convocation with Convocation Gown & Hat!

INDIVIDUAL SEGMENT 02

2. English Public Speaking

Topic Announcement

The official speech topic will be announced 1 day prior to competition day.

Stage Performance

On event day, each speaker gets exactly 3 minutes on stage to deliver their English speech.

Evaluation Criteria

Judges score based on Pronunciation, Accent Accuracy, Structure of Speech, Literary Depth, and Stage Presence.

INDIVIDUAL SEGMENT 03

3. Literature Writing (Story & Poetry)

Submission Format

Participants submit a Short Story (1000 words or fewer) or a Poem (50 lines or fewer) before the designated submission deadline (2–3 days prior to the event).

AI & Plagiarism Screening

Submissions undergo automated plagiarism and AI checks. Up to 20%–25% minor AI traces are tolerated; higher AI content results in immediate disqualification.

Concept Defense & Stage Recitation

Participants explain their concept to the panel of judges on event day. The winning entries will be recited live on stage by professional storytellers!

TEAM SEGMENT 04

4. Team Case Solving Competition

Case Release & Team Formation

Participate in teams. The problem case study statement is released prior to the event day.

Poster Board Design

Teams design physical presentation boards featuring hand-drawn graphics, infographics, and structured data tables.

3-Minute Stage Pitch

Teams deliver a 3-minute pitch on stage. Champions and Runners-up receive Official Trophies and Crests for all members!
