WHO WE ARE

More Than An Organisation.
A Vision For Every Student.

ABILITY is an educational organization dedicated to recognising excellence, creating opportunities and inspiring the next generation of students across Bangladesh.

We believe talent exists in every district, classroom, and student. Our mission is to bridge the opportunity gap through academic championships, rural educational development, science laboratories and future-ready skill programmes.
